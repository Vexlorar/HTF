  • Content involving the sexual exploitation of minors
  • Illegal products or services
  • Fraudulent schemes
  • Scams
  • Phishing services
  • Malware or malicious software
  • Credential theft

  • Counterfeit products

Advertising Content Requirements
Advertisers are responsible for ensuring that the materials they provide are accurate and lawful.
