We're Here to Help
Welcome to the APXMOD Contact Center.
Whether you have a question about our platform, need assistance with an application or game, want to report an issue, or have a concern regarding content published on our website, our team is available to review your request.
APXMOD is operated by a dedicated team, and incoming requests may be reviewed by the appropriate team member depending on the nature of the inquiry.
We aim to handle legitimate requests carefully, professionally, and as efficiently as possible.
Contact Our Team
For general questions, technical assistance, content-related concerns, feedback, or other inquiries, please contact us at:
support@apxmod.com
When contacting us, please provide enough information for our team to understand and investigate your request.
What Can You Contact Us About?
We handle a variety of requests across different areas of the platform. Below are some of the most common reasons users contact our team.
General Support
For general questions about APXMOD, our platform, available features, accounts, or other website-related matters, you can contact our support team.
Examples include:
  • Questions about using the website
  • Account-related questions
  • Questions about platform features
  • General website assistance
  • Feedback and suggestions
Application & Game Issues
If you experience a problem with an application or game listed on APXMOD, please contact us.
Where possible, include:
  • Application or game name
  • Version
  • The page URL
  • Description of the problem
  • Your Android version
  • Relevant screenshots or other useful information
Providing these details can help our team investigate the issue more efficiently.
If your question is about installing an application or game, you may find an immediate answer in our How to Install guide, where the most common questions and their answers are provided so you can get help right away before contacting us.
Security Concerns
If you believe that a file or application available through APXMOD may present a security concern, please report it to us as soon as possible.
Please include:
  • Application or game name
  • Relevant page
  • Version or file information
  • Description of the security concern
  • Any supporting evidence available to you
Security-related reports may be forwarded to the appropriate members of our technical or review team for investigation.
Content Corrections
If you identify information that appears to be incorrect, outdated, incomplete, or misleading, we encourage you to let us know.
This may include incorrect:
  • Application information
  • Version information
  • File size
  • Android requirements
  • Developer information
  • Modification attribution
  • Description
  • Features
  • Other listing details
Our team may review the information and make appropriate corrections where the information can be reasonably verified.
Copyright & DMCA Requests
If you are a copyright holder or authorized representative and believe that content available through APXMOD infringes your rights, you can contact us regarding the matter.
Please provide sufficient information to allow our team to understand and evaluate the request.
Copyright-related requests are handled through our DMCA Notice procedure and may require specific information or documentation.
For detailed instructions, please refer to our DMCA Notice page.
User Account & Community Concerns
If you experience an issue involving your APXMOD account, user reviews, or other community-related features, you can contact our support team.
Please provide relevant information such as your account details and a clear description of the issue.
Important: For privacy and security reasons, never send your password, authentication codes, payment credentials, or other sensitive account information by email.
How to Contact Us
Reach our support team directly by email:
support@apxmod.com
Recommended Email Format
To help us process your request efficiently, we recommend using a clear subject line. For example:
Subject: Application Issue — [Application Name]
Subject: Security Concern — [Application Name]
Subject: Copyright / DMCA Request — [Content Name]
Subject: General Support — APXMOD
In your message, briefly explain the issue and provide any relevant information or evidence.
Response & Review
Every legitimate request may require a different level of investigation.
General questions may be straightforward to address, while technical, security, copyright, or content-related matters may require additional review by the appropriate members of our team.
We aim to review and respond to legitimate inquiries within 24 hours where reasonably possible.
More complex requests may require additional time when verification, investigation, or supporting information is necessary.
Before Contacting Us
To help us assist you more effectively, please:
  • Clearly describe your issue or request
  • Include the relevant application, game, or page
  • Provide screenshots when they help explain the problem
  • Include relevant technical information for technical issues
  • Provide supporting documentation for rights-related requests where applicable
  • Avoid sending passwords or other sensitive credentials
Incomplete requests may require additional communication before our team can properly investigate the matter.
Responsible Communication
We welcome genuine questions, feedback, reports, and concerns.
To help our team process requests efficiently, please ensure that communications are accurate, relevant, and respectful.
We reserve the right to disregard communications that are abusive, threatening, intentionally misleading, spam, or unrelated to the operation of APXMOD.
Our Commitment
At APXMOD, communication with our users is an important part of maintaining the platform.
Whether you are reporting a problem, requesting a correction, raising a security concern, providing feedback, or simply asking a question, your message helps our team understand how we can continue improving the platform.
We aim to provide a professional, transparent, and responsive support experience as APXMOD continues to grow.
