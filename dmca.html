DMCA Notice
How to submit a copyright infringement report and how APXMOD reviews legitimate DMCA complaints.
APXMOD respects the intellectual property rights of copyright owners, developers, publishers, creators, and other rights holders. We take legitimate copyright concerns seriously and are committed to reviewing properly submitted copyright complaints in a responsible and timely manner.
If you believe that copyrighted material available through APXMOD infringes your copyright, or the copyright of a person or organization you are authorized to represent, you may submit a copyright infringement notice to our dedicated DMCA team.
dmca@apxmod.com
Section 1
Copyright Infringement Reports
A copyright owner or an authorized representative may contact APXMOD if they believe that specific material available through our platform infringes their copyright.
Because a copyright complaint may result in content being restricted or removed, we require clear, relevant, and reasonably verifiable evidence supporting the claim.
A general statement such as "This is my content" or "I own this application" may not, by itself, provide sufficient information for us to properly evaluate the complaint.
We therefore encourage rights holders to provide appropriate documentation and evidence establishing their ownership or authorization.
Section 2
Who Can Submit a DMCA Notice?
A DMCA notice should generally be submitted by:
  • The copyright owner; or
  • A person or organization legally authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
If you are submitting a complaint on behalf of another person, developer, publisher, company, or organization, please clearly explain your relationship to the rights holder and provide appropriate evidence of your authority where applicable.
Section 3
Information Required in a DMCA Notice
To help our team properly investigate a copyright complaint, please provide the following information.
3.1 Copyright Owner Information
Please provide:
  • Full name of the copyright owner
  • Company or organization name, if applicable
  • Contact email address
  • Official website or other reliable identification
  • Your relationship to the copyrighted work
  • Confirmation that you are the copyright owner or authorized representative
3.2 Identification of the Copyrighted Work
Please clearly identify the copyrighted work that you believe has been infringed.
Depending on the circumstances, useful evidence may include:
  • Official application or game name
  • Official website
  • Official store listing
  • Original publication information
  • Original release information
  • Developer or publisher information
  • Copyright registration information, where applicable
  • Licensing or ownership documentation
  • Original publication records
  • Other reliable evidence establishing ownership
For applications or games, please provide enough information for our team to distinguish the original work from other similar content.
Section 4
Identification of the Reported Material
Please provide the exact location of the material you are reporting on APXMOD.
Where possible, include:
  • APXMOD page URL
  • Application or game name
  • Version number
  • Relevant download page
  • Specific material being challenged
  • Screenshots, where useful
  • Any additional URLs associated with the reported material
If multiple pages or files are involved, please list each relevant URL separately.
Section 5
Supporting Evidence
Adequate supporting evidence is extremely important.
Before taking action against a listing or restricting access to material, our team may need to establish that the reporting party has legitimate rights or authorization concerning the copyrighted work.
Examples of useful supporting evidence may include:
  • Official developer or publisher website
  • Official application or game store listing
  • Copyright registration documentation
  • Ownership documentation
  • Licensing documentation
  • Publishing agreements
  • Original source files or publication records
  • Official documentation identifying the rights holder
  • Documentation establishing authorization to act on behalf of the rights holder
  • Other reliable evidence demonstrating ownership or relevant rights
The evidence should directly relate to the material being reported.
If the evidence provided is insufficient, our team may request additional information before making a final determination.
Section 6
Proof of Authority
If you are not the copyright owner, you should provide evidence demonstrating that you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder.
This may include appropriate authorization from:
  • The copyright owner
  • Developer
  • Publisher
  • Rights holder
  • Authorized legal representative
  • Relevant organization
A person who is not authorized to represent the rights holder should not submit a copyright complaint on that party's behalf.
Section 7
Good-Faith Requirement
Copyright complaints should be submitted only when the reporting party genuinely believes that the identified material infringes their copyright or rights they are authorized to enforce.
The information provided should be accurate and truthful to the best of the reporting party's knowledge.
We may reject or request clarification regarding notices that appear to be:
  • Fraudulent
  • Intentionally misleading
  • Unsupported
  • Incomplete
  • Submitted by an unauthorized person
  • Based solely on an unsupported ownership claim
  • Intended to improperly remove lawful material
  • Submitted for harassment, retaliation, or abuse
Repeated submission of knowingly false or abusive complaints may result in appropriate action.
Section 8
Required Declaration
A properly submitted copyright notice should include a statement confirming that:
  • The reporting party genuinely believes the reported use of the material is unauthorized or infringing; and
  • The information contained in the notice is accurate to the best of the reporting party's knowledge; and
  • The reporting party is the copyright owner or is authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
Section 9
Electronic Signature
The notice should include the physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
For an electronic submission, a typed full legal name may be used as an electronic signature where appropriate.
Section 10
Review Process
Once a copyright complaint is received, our team may review:
  • The identity of the reported material.
  • The APXMOD page or URL identified in the complaint.
  • The identity of the reporting party.
  • Evidence of copyright ownership.
  • Evidence of authorization, where applicable.
  • The relationship between the reported material and the copyrighted work.
  • Available developer, publisher, source, and attribution information.
  • Any additional documentation supplied.
  • Whether further information is necessary to evaluate the complaint.
We may contact the reporting party for clarification or additional evidence when necessary.
Section 11
Content Restriction or Removal
If a copyright complaint is properly submitted and sufficiently supported, APXMOD may, where appropriate and subject to applicable law:
  • Remove the reported content;
  • Disable or restrict access to the reported content;
  • Remove or restrict a download page;
  • Restrict access to a reported application or game listing;
  • Remove associated copyrighted material; or
  • Take other appropriate action concerning the reported material.
However, submission of a complaint does not automatically establish copyright infringement.
Our team may review the available information and supporting evidence before taking action.
Where the available information reasonably establishes a legitimate copyright concern, we may take appropriate action without unnecessary delay.
Section 12
24-Hour Response Target
We understand that copyright concerns can be time-sensitive.
APXMOD aims to provide an initial response or acknowledgment to properly submitted DMCA reports within 24 hours where reasonably possible.
Please understand that this is a response target and does not necessarily mean that every investigation, verification, or removal decision will be completed within 24 hours.
Complex cases may require additional time because of:
  • Ownership verification
  • Insufficient documentation
  • Multiple reported URLs
  • Conflicting claims
  • Requests for additional evidence
  • Communication with relevant parties
  • Legal or technical considerations
Providing complete and clear documentation can help us process a complaint more efficiently.
Section 13
Counter-Notification and Disputed Claims
If material has been restricted or removed following a copyright complaint, the affected party may contact APXMOD if they believe the action was taken in error or that they have the necessary rights or authorization to use the material.
A response should clearly explain:
  • The material affected
  • Why the removal or restriction is disputed
  • The legal or factual basis for the dispute
  • Evidence supporting the right or authorization to use the material
  • Relevant contact information
Where applicable and required by law, APXMOD may consider a valid counter-notification or other appropriate response.
Depending on the circumstances, additional information or legal clarification may be required before content is restored.
Section 14
Repeat Copyright Infringement
APXMOD may take appropriate action against users or accounts that repeatedly distribute material determined to infringe copyright.
Depending on the circumstances, this may include:
  • Content removal
  • Feature restrictions
  • Temporary account suspension
  • Permanent account termination
  • Other appropriate measures consistent with applicable law
Section 15
Third-Party Applications and Games
APXMOD may research and present applications, games, modifications, and related information originating from various publicly available online sources, including international forums, public communities, and other internet sources.
The presence of an application or game on APXMOD does not automatically mean that APXMOD is its developer, publisher, copyright owner, or modifier.
Where reliable information is available, we may identify:
  • Developer / Original Creator
  • Modified / Modded By
  • Original Source / Attribution
  • Play Store or Official Store Link
  • Other relevant information
APXMOD does not claim ownership of third-party applications or games merely because they are listed or presented on the platform.
Section 16
Copyright Attribution Concerns
If you believe that the attribution associated with an application, game, modification, or other material is incorrect, please contact our DMCA team with appropriate evidence.
Please include:
  • The relevant APXMOD URL
  • The incorrect attribution
  • The correct ownership or attribution information
  • Supporting evidence
  • Your relationship to the relevant rights holder
Our team may review the information and make appropriate corrections where reasonably verified.
Section 17
Copyright Complaints vs. Other Legal Complaints
This DMCA process is intended primarily for copyright-related complaints.
If your concern relates to another issue, please clearly identify the nature of your complaint.
Examples include:
  • Privacy concerns
  • Security vulnerabilities
  • Trademark disputes
  • Account issues
  • User misconduct
  • Incorrect information
  • Other legal concerns
For general support or non-DMCA matters:
support@apxmod.com
For copyright and DMCA matters:
dmca@apxmod.com
Section 18
Trademark and Other Intellectual Property Claims
Not every intellectual-property dispute is a copyright matter.
If your complaint concerns:
  • Trademark infringement
  • Brand misuse
  • False affiliation
  • Patent rights
  • Other intellectual-property rights
please clearly identify the specific right involved and provide appropriate supporting documentation.
Our team may request additional information or direct the matter to the appropriate process where necessary.
Section 19
False, Fraudulent, or Abusive Notices
Copyright reporting mechanisms must not be used to improperly remove legitimate content or to harass another party.
APXMOD does not support knowingly false or fraudulent copyright claims.
This includes:
  • False ownership claims
  • Forged documentation
  • Misleading evidence
  • False statements of authorization
  • Deliberately inaccurate information
  • Complaints submitted for harassment or retaliation
  • Attempts to remove legitimate content without a valid rights-based claim
Where appropriate, APXMOD may take action regarding demonstrably fraudulent or abusive submissions.
Section 20
Information You Should Not Send
When contacting our DMCA team, please do not unnecessarily include:
  • Passwords
  • Authentication codes
  • Banking credentials
  • Payment card information
  • Private account credentials
  • Unrelated sensitive personal information
Provide only information reasonably necessary to establish and process your copyright claim.
Section 21
How to Submit a DMCA Notice
All copyright infringement notices should be sent to our dedicated DMCA email:
dmca@apxmod.com
For faster processing, please use a subject such as:
Subject: DMCA Copyright Infringement Notice — [Application/Game/Content Name]
Your email should ideally contain the following:
Copyright Owner Full legal name and organization, if applicable.
Authorized Representative Your full name and your relationship to the copyright owner, if applicable.
Contact Information A valid email address and other appropriate contact information.
Copyrighted Work A clear description of the original copyrighted work.
Ownership Evidence Official links, documentation, registration information, publication records, or other relevant evidence.
Reported APXMOD Content Exact URL(s) and a clear description of the material being reported.
Proof of Authority Documentation establishing that you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder, where applicable.
Supporting Evidence Screenshots, official documentation, source information, ownership records, or other relevant evidence.
Accuracy and Authority Statement A statement confirming that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge and that you are the copyright owner or authorized representative.
Electronic Signature Your full legal name as an electronic signature.
Section 22
What Happens After a Report?
The general review process may follow these stages:
Receive Report ↓ Review the Submitted Information ↓ Verify Ownership / Authorization ↓ Examine the Reported Material ↓ Request Additional Evidence if Necessary ↓ Determine Appropriate Action ↓ Notify the Relevant Party
Depending on the circumstances, APXMOD may:
  • Request additional documentation
  • Request clarification
  • Restrict reported material during an appropriate review where warranted
  • Remove or disable access to material where appropriate
  • Reject an unsupported complaint
  • Consider a valid counter-notification
  • Take other appropriate action
The exact process may vary depending on the nature and circumstances of the complaint.
Section 23
Important Notice Regarding Evidence
Because removing an application, game, file, or other material can significantly affect its availability, APXMOD may require sufficient evidence before taking permanent action.
We therefore encourage rights holders to provide strong, relevant, and verifiable evidence with their initial report.
Examples of particularly useful evidence include official developer or publisher pages, official store listings, ownership documentation, copyright registration information where applicable, licensing records, and evidence establishing your authority to act.
The stronger and clearer the evidence, the more effectively our team can evaluate the complaint.
